## 🔬 About Me

I am a computational biologist and data scientist working at the intersection of genomics, multi-omics, quantitative pathology, and translational medicine. My research focuses on using high-dimensional biological data to understand disease mechanisms in brain tumors, vascular disease, and intracranial aneurysms.

I trained in both Molecular Biology, Genetics and Bioengineering and Computer Science and Engineering at Sabancı University, then continued my research training at Yale School of Medicine in the Günel Lab. At Yale, I worked on cancer and vascular disease genetics, single-cell and spatial omics, patient-derived organoid models, and computational pathology.

My current goal is to develop transparent, reusable, and clinically grounded computational pipelines that connect patient-derived specimens to mechanistic and testable biological hypotheses.

## 🧪 Research Themes

### Brain Tumor Genomics

I work on mapping genetic alterations to histopathologic features in brain tumors, especially meningiomas. My work has included integrated analysis of whole-exome sequencing, whole-genome sequencing, RNA-seq, immunohistochemistry, and quantitative pathology to characterize molecularly defined tumor subgroups.

### Intracranial Aneurysm Biology

I study vascular disease through single-cell, spatial, and immune profiling approaches. My work has included CyTOF-based immune characterization of endoluminal biopsy samples, spatial proteomics and transcriptomics of aneurysm tissues, and tissue-level analyses related to aneurysm progression and rupture.

### Patient-Derived Organoid Models

I contribute to patient-derived brain tumor organoid studies by connecting histology, multi-omics, and treatment response assays. This work aims to build higher-throughput systems for studying tumor biology and therapy response in patient-anchored models.

### Translational Data Science

My long-term interest is to build computational tools that are rigorous, interpretable, and useful for clinicians and experimental collaborators. I am especially interested in pipelines that move from high-dimensional data to biological mechanism and clinical insight.

## 🧩 Selected Projects

### FOS/FOSB Fusion-Driven Meningiomas

Integrated genomic and histopathologic analysis to characterize a newly defined subgroup of meningiomas involving FOS and FOSB gene fusions.

🔗 Project release: [ErsonLab/FOS_FOSB_Paper v1.2](https://github.com/ErsonLab/FOS_FOSB_Paper/releases/tag/v1.2)

### Single-Cell Immune Profiling of Intracranial Aneurysms

Used CyTOF-based single-cell proteomics to characterize immune populations in aneurysm biopsy samples and study inflammatory features associated with disease states.

### Spatial Multi-Omics of Human Intracranial Aneurysms

Contributed to spatial proteomics, spatial transcriptomics, and histophenotypic analysis of human aneurysm tissues to study tissue-level changes and rupture-associated biology.

### Brain Tumor Organoid Modeling

Worked on patient-derived organoid models for meningioma, glioma, and schwannoma, including histological validation, quantitative pathology, and multi-omics screening.

### PathwayVis

Developed a clinical bioinformatics tool to visualize mutated pathways in tumor samples and support molecular tumor board interpretation.

🔗 GitHub repository: [BaturGultekin/pathwayVis](https://github.com/BaturGultekin/pathwayVis)

### XR-seq Based Transcription Event Discovery

Built a computational tool to detect potential novel transcription events in *Arabidopsis thaliana* using XR-seq data.

🔗 GitHub repository: [BaturGultekin/Exploring-Novel-Transcription-Events](https://github.com/BaturGultekin/Exploring-Novel-Transcription-Events)
